Pressure performance

High-pressure deliveries use a pressure-index threshold of 0.70. Batting measures pressure strike rate, strike-rate lift over the player's own baseline, boundary rate, dismissal rate and dot-ball rate. Bowling measures pressure economy, wickets per 100 legal balls, dot-ball rate, economy improvement and boundary-concession rate. Percentile-based components include a sample-size reliability adjustment.

Injury / fitness

Assesses injury frequency, severity and recovery duration, recurrence, recent injury status and recovery, workload and tournament availability. Higher score means lower risk. A score of 50 is a neutral placeholder where verified player-specific evidence is incomplete; it is not confirmation of fitness. Pathirana (20) and Hasaranga (25) are provisional risk discounts, not medical diagnoses.

RR fit

Analyst assessment of role requirement, squad depth, Indian/overseas balance and likely contribution. This is a decision-support judgement, not a model-generated historical statistic.

Eligibility and QA

  • Released does not mean registered or guaranteed to attend the auction.
  • Sikandar Raza and Mark Chapman are uncontracted prospects; auction registration is unconfirmed.
  • Confirm final registration, player availability, eligibility and late trades.
  • Some pressure evidence is T20I-only or combined; international-only evidence needs cautious IPL translation.
  • RR fit is not a bid price or probability of winning a bid.
