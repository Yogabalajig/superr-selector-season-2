Methodology & Decision Guardrails

Pressure Performance (80%)

Primary evidence layer: pressure performance score from IPL/T20I analysis. Pressure rankings remain provisional and depend on dataset definitions and thresholds.

Injury / Fitness (10%)

Historical injury frequency 25%; severity/recovery 20%; recurrence 20%; recent status 20%; workload/availability 15%.

RR Squad Fit (10%)

Role alignment 30%; skill/phase contribution 25%; squad balance/slot economics 20%; role scarcity 15%; versatility 10%.

Formula

Final = 0.80 × PP + 0.10 × Injury + 0.10 × RR Fit

PP + Injury lens = 0.90 × PP + 0.10 × Injury.

Important score caveat: Injury score 50 is a neutral placeholder where reliable player-specific evidence is incomplete; it is not a medical clearance. Pathirana (20) and Hasaranga (25) are provisional risk discounts pending medical-grade verification. No player should be treated as auction-available until registration and availability are confirmed. Rankings are analyst decision support and should be revisited against final auction list, squad composition, budget and medical checks.

Pressure score limitations

Pressure cut-off and scoring weights are experimental. Scores combine competition-normalized evidence where available. Sensitivity testing showed moderate batter and higher bowler ranking sensitivity to the pressure threshold; use ranks alongside score gaps and role context.
