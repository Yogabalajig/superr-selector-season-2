RR Fit

Role/squad need 30%; phase contribution 25%; balance/slot economics 20%; scarcity 15%; versatility 10%.

Fitness evidence

Injury frequency 25%; severity/recovery 20%; recurrence 20%; recent status 20%; workload/availability 15%. Neutral 50 denotes unverified, not medically cleared.
